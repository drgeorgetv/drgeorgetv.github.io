Dr. George T. Varghese, MD

Clinical AI product builder and evaluator

Medical doctor with an MD in Pharmacology and hands-on experience in medical affairs and applied AI. I worked as a Medical Science Liaison at Bristol Myers Squibb, a US-headquartered biopharma, engaging over 70 physicians across South India, and I built KallosumAI, a digital health platform that helps pharma teams synthesize medical evidence.

I build with LLMs and RAG, and I design rigorous evaluations of AI outputs, assessing correctness, reasoning quality, and suitability for real-world use in regulated healthcare settings. I combine hands-on building with clinical domain depth, commercial awareness, and a focus on turning ideas into working tools.
